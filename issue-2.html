Issue #2: How to get an accurate quote the first time

Most quote surprises come from missing details.

  • Send photos and rough measurements.
  • Mention stairs, long carries, pets and parking.
  • Ask what is not included (disposal fees, materials, travel).
  • Get the scope in writing and compare at least two quotes line by line.

Use the free price calculator and cost guides to sanity-check numbers.
