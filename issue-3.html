Issue #3: Rent, buy, or hire? Carpet cleaners and pressure washers

Before buying equipment, compare three options:

  • Rent for one-off jobs: no storage or maintenance.
  • Buy if you'll use it several times a year; see our spec comparisons.
  • Hire for large, high, or delicate surfaces (roofs, second stories, wool rugs), where mistakes cost more than the service.

Safety reminder: never use a pressure washer from a ladder, and keep the nozzle away from people, pets and windows.
