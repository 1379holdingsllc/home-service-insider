Issue #1: The fall exterior checklist

Fall is the best time to handle the exterior before winter weather.

  1. Gutters: clean after most leaves drop; flush downspouts and confirm water exits away from the foundation.
  2. Pressure washing: wash siding and concrete before temperatures stay below freezing so water doesn't sit in cracks.
  3. Deck: check for soft boards and loose railings; reseal only when wood is dry.
  4. Weatherstripping: a candle or incense near closed doors shows drafts.

Deal-hunting tip: many exterior contractors are less busy late in the season; ask whether bundling gutters + pressure washing in one visit lowers the trip charge.
